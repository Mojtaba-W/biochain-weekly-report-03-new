Fruit peels and plant cuticles offer design strategies for functional packaging barriers and improved protection of food products.

Learning from Gecko Adhesion

Hierarchical surfaces and medical adhesives

Bio-inspired adhesion
BMS-18-2026

Can gecko-inspired surfaces help adhesives work in medical settings?

Hierarchical surface structures can inspire medical adhesives, but performance on wet, delicate tissue requires additional engineering and testing.

Biomimicry lens

Gecko toe pads use multiscale structures that support attachment across surfaces. Translating this principle to medical adhesives means accounting for moisture, tissue sensitivity, removal, sterilization, and biocompatibility.

Engineering question

How can an adhesive maintain reliable attachment on wet tissue while minimizing damage during removal?

Thursday · Biotechnology

Learning from Evolution

Improving enzymes through directed evolution

Enzyme engineering
BMS-19-2026

How can evolution help scientists improve industrial enzymes?

Directed evolution uses cycles of variation and selection to develop enzymes with useful properties for industrial applications.

تکامل هدایت‌شده با چرخه‌های ایجاد تنوع و انتخاب، به بهبود ویژگی‌های آنزیم‌ها برای کاربردهای صنعتی کمک می‌کند.

Read on LinkedIn ↗

Biomimicry lens

Natural evolution explores variation under selection. Directed evolution adapts this logic in the laboratory: generate variants, screen or select for a target property, and repeat to improve performance.

Engineering question

How can researchers define a useful selection pressure that improves enzyme performance under real industrial conditions?

Interactions among plants, microorganisms, substrates, and water can guide the design of ecological treatment systems.

The week’s scientific lesson

From biological observation to engineered solution

Across five fields, biomimicry follows a disciplined pathway: identify what nature does, understand how the function works, abstract the principle, and test whether the engineered result performs in its intended context.

01 · ObserveNotice a biological strategy
02 · UnderstandIdentify the function
03 · ExtractAbstract the principle
04 · EngineerTranslate into a design
05 · TestMeasure real performance
🌿
Nature offers strategies—not ready-made technologies.Using biology is not automatically biomimicry. The key is intentional translation of biological strategies into design principles, followed by rigorous testing.
